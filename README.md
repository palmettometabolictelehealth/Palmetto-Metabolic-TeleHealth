Palmetto Metabolic TeleHealth provides evidence‑based metabolic care, chronic condition management, and primary care telehealth to patients across South Carolina. Our clinicians deliver confidential, convenient, and personalized virtual care designed to fit real life — helping individuals achieve sustainable health change with science‑driven support.

We serve communities throughout Columbia, Lexington, Charleston, Greenville, Horry County, and surrounding regions, expanding access to high‑quality healthcare for both urban and rural patients.

Our clinical services include:  
• Metabolic health & weight management
• Chronic disease support (hypertension, diabetes, thyroid conditions, and more)
• Primary care telehealth
• Personalized treatment plans
• Secure virtual visits and ongoing support

Our mission is to make trusted, local telehealth accessible to every South Carolinian seeking better metabolic health, improved chronic care, and whole‑body wellbeing.
