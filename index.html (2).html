Meet the Founder

Tysha Holmes, PA-C, MPH/MBA

Physician Assistant-Certified · Healthcare Entrepreneur · Columbia, SC

I'm Tysha Holmes — and I built Palmetto Metabolic TeleHealth because I know what it feels like to search for answers, feel unheard, and try to navigate weight, hormones, and metabolic health without clear guidance.

I've been the patient sitting across from providers who were too busy to listen. That experience shaped every part of how I practice today.

With over 25 years of clinical experience across primary care, metabolic medicine, and chronic condition management, I've seen firsthand what happens when people don't have access to compassionate, evidence-based care — and what's possible when they do.

Palmetto Metabolic TeleHealth grew from that personal experience. What started as a seed — a desire to help people feel seen, supported, and empowered — has become a full-service metabolic and primary care practice serving individuals and families across Columbia, Lexington, and the greater South Carolina community.

We combine clinical expertise with a human-centered approach, offering telehealth, personalized treatment plans, and ongoing support that meets people exactly where they are. Every service we provide is rooted in one belief: your health should feel approachable, not overwhelming — and you deserve a provider who listens.

Palmetto Metabolic TeleHealth is still blooming — and so are the people we serve.

What We Stand For

The Values Behind Every Visit

These aren't just words on a wall — they're the principles that guide every patient interaction, every treatment decision, and every follow-up we have.

Patient First, Always

Every decision we make starts with one question: what's best for this patient? Your goals, your history, and your life shape the care you receive — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

Evidence-Based Care

We don't follow fads. Every treatment plan we create is grounded in current medical evidence and clinical best practices — because you deserve care that actually works.

Compassion Without Judgment

We understand that weight and metabolic health are deeply personal. You'll never feel judged here — only supported, respected, and encouraged toward sustainable progress.

Privacy & Security

Your health information is sacred. Our platform is fully HIPAA-secure, so you can share openly with your care team knowing your data is protected at every step.

Accessibility & Convenience

Quality care shouldn't require a day off work or a long drive. Our virtual-first model means expert care is available wherever you are in South Carolina.

Long-Term Results

We're not here for quick fixes. We're here for lasting transformation — ongoing monitoring, regular check-ins, and care that evolves with you over time.
